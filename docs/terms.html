DHARM YT PRO · Last updated: November 2026

What the service is

DHARM YT PRO is a scheduling tool for channel owners. With the owner's Google OAuth permission it takes videos the owner placed in their own Google Drive folder, uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel as private videos, and schedules them to publish at set times.

Your responsibilities

  • You only connect channels and Drive folders that you own or are authorised to manage.
  • You only upload content you have the rights to. You follow the YouTube Terms of Service and YouTube Community Guidelines, including the spam and deceptive-practices policies.
  • You review titles, descriptions and hashtags written with AI help. You are responsible for what is published.

Google and YouTube

By using this service you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. How this service uses your data is in our Privacy Policy.

Access and removal

You can stop the service at any time by removing its access at Google Account → Third-party access. On request, the channel and its encrypted tokens are deleted from the backend.

No warranty

The service is provided as is. Uploads depend on Google's APIs, quotas and processing, which can fail or be delayed.
